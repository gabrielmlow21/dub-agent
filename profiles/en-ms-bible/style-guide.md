# Style guide: Bahasa Melayu (Bible content)

## Variety
- Malaysian Malay (Bahasa Malaysia), never Indonesian spellings or vocabulary. Check doubtful words with DBP / PRPM (https://prpm.dbp.gov.my).

## Bible text
- Quote verses word for word from the Alkitab Versi Borneo (AVB) at alkitab.sabda.org (version "AVB"). Don't translate them yourself.
- A client may have its own word swaps on top of the AVB text. List them here, in the order they must be applied: a swap done in the wrong order can undo another one.
- Book names and capitals follow the AVB (e.g. "TUHAN" for "the LORD").

## Voice text (what the TTS reads)
- Write verse references the way they're said: "Mazmur 1:3" → "Mazmur 1 ayat 3".
- Spell out anything the voice should read letter by letter, with hyphens: "A-B-C".
- Leave out production cues ("[ PART 1 ]", "—END—").

## Formatting
- Dates: 8 Oktober 2026. Currency: RM10.00.
- Keep brand and product names in the source language.
